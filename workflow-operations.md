# Property Investment Intelligence Platform

## Phase 12 – Workflow Automation & Property Operations Platform

---

# Introduction

The previous phases focused on helping investors:

* Discover opportunities
* Analyse investments
* Assess risks
* Review legal packs
* Manage portfolios
* Track wealth
* Generate reports

At this stage the platform provides exceptional visibility and decision support.

However, investors still perform many repetitive manual activities.

Examples:

* Uploading legal packs
* Reviewing documents
* Creating tasks
* Assigning work
* Generating reports
* Following up on risks
* Tracking due diligence

As portfolios grow, these manual processes become difficult to manage.

The next evolution of the platform is automation.

Instead of simply providing information, the platform begins performing work automatically.

This phase introduces a complete Workflow Automation and Property Operations Platform.

The goal is to reduce manual effort and increase operational efficiency.

---

# Business Objectives

The platform should help investors answer:

### What should happen next?

### Can the platform do it automatically?

### Can repetitive work be eliminated?

### Can risks be monitored automatically?

### Can workflows be standardised?

The objective is to automate operational processes wherever possible.
