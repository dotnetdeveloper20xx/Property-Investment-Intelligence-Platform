# Introduction

The previous phases transformed the platform into a comprehensive Property Investment Intelligence Platform.

The platform can now:

* Discover opportunities
* Analyse investments
* Review legal packs
* Assess risks
* Manage portfolios
* Generate reports
* Automate workflows
* Monitor investments
* Gather market intelligence
* Acquire external property data

At this stage the platform is highly intelligent.

However, it still primarily focuses on understanding the present.

The next evolution is prediction.

Professional investors are constantly asking:

### What is likely to happen next?

### Which opportunities will outperform?

### Which locations will grow?

### Which investments are likely to fail?
