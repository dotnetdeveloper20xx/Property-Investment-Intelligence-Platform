# Property Investment Intelligence Platform

## Phase 13 – Notifications, Alerts & Monitoring Platform

---

# Introduction

The previous phases transformed the platform into a powerful property investment operating system.

The platform can now:

* Discover opportunities
* Analyse investments
* Process legal packs
* Generate AI recommendations
* Calculate risk scores
* Manage portfolios
* Track wealth
* Generate reports
* Automate workflows

However, one major problem still exists.

The platform remains largely reactive.

Users must:

* Log in
* Open dashboards
* Review reports
* Check portfolios
* Search for issues

Professional investors do not want to constantly check systems.

They want systems that monitor their investments and notify them when something important happens.

This phase introduces the Notifications, Alerts, and Monitoring Platform.

The goal is to transform the platform into an active investment assistant.

Instead of waiting for investors to discover issues, the platform proactively identifies, monitors, and communicates important events.

---

# Business Objectives

The monitoring platform should answer:

### What requires my attention today?

### What risks have emerged?

### What opportunities have appeared?

The platform should continuously watch investments and notify users about significant changes.
