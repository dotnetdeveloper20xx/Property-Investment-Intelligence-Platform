# Property Investment Intelligence Platform

## Phase 9 – Deal Vault, Investor CRM & Collaboration Platform

---

# Introduction

The previous phases transformed the platform into a comprehensive property investment ecosystem.

The platform can now:

* Discover opportunities
* Manage opportunities
* Analyse investments
* Process legal packs
* Generate AI recommendations
* Calculate risk scores
* Analyse markets
* Manage portfolios
* Track wealth growth

At this stage an individual investor can successfully use the platform to manage their investment journey.

However, professional property businesses face additional challenges.

Many property professionals review hundreds or thousands of opportunities every year.

Examples include:

* Property sourcers
* Deal packaging businesses
* Investment companies
* Property partnerships
* Family offices
* Portfolio managers
* Acquisitions teams

These businesses need far more than analysis.

They need organisation.

They need collaboration.

They need institutional memory.

They need research management.

Most businesses currently manage this information across:

* Excel spreadsheets
* Shared drives
* Emails
* WhatsApp groups
* Notion
* Trello
* CRM systems

The result is fragmented knowledge and duplicated effort.

Phase 9 introduces the Deal Vault and Investor CRM.

This transforms the platform into an operational workspace for serious investors and investment teams.

# The Deal Vault

The Deal Vault becomes one of the most important business assets within the platform.

Every opportunity analysed throughout the lifetime of the business is stored permanently.

Instead of losing knowledge, investors build an investment intelligence library.

# Why The Deal Vault Matters

Professional investors often ask:

Why did we reject it?

Who reviewed it?

What risks were identified?

Most businesses cannot answer these questions easily.

The Deal Vault solves this problem.
