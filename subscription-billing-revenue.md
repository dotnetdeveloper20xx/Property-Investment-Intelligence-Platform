---

# Introduction

The previous phases focused on building a powerful Property Investment Intelligence Platform.

The platform now provides:

* Opportunity Management
* Investment Analysis
* Legal Intelligence
* AI Intelligence
* Risk Intelligence
* Market Intelligence
* Portfolio Management
* Workflow Automation
* Reporting
* Monitoring
* Enterprise Administration

At this point the platform is technically capable of delivering significant value.

However, delivering value is only one part of building a successful software business.

The second part is generating sustainable revenue.

This phase introduces the Subscription, Billing, and Revenue Platform.

The goal is to transform the platform from a software product into a commercially viable SaaS business.
